Ghost words come from the model's own numbers
One pass per generated token, inside the worker, in src/worker/ghost-words.ts.
1 · The model speaks

Logits

At each step GPT-2 produces one score (a logit) for every token in its vocabulary. A logits processor sees them before the next token is drawn.

2 · Probabilities

Softmax over everything

The step's logits are divided by the temperature (0.7) and turned into probabilities over the full vocabulary. The best eight are kept, so unreadable tokens can be skipped.

3 · The same distribution

Drawn from the same

The library's temperature stays at 1 and top_k is 0, so it does not narrow the choice to 50 tokens: what is shown is what was sampled from.

4 · On the altar

Three ghosts per word

The chosen token is removed; up to three alternatives remain, fainter, each with its probability on hover and focus.

Why top_k: 0 matters

transformers.js samples from the 50 best tokens by default and renormalises them. Showing probabilities over the whole vocabulary while sampling from 50 would be a small lie, caught in review and fixed. Now the two agree.

What is never invented

  • a ghost word is always one of the model's own most likely tokens at that step
  • the one-line list of blocked words only removes tokens; it adds none
  • screen readers get "almost: X, Y, Z" as text, outside the live answer
  • the first word of an answer can show the end-of-text token, because it was a candidate
