The ritual: oldest voice first
A fixed sequence in plain code. The models only generate text; they never decide the order.

An offering: one of six offered questions (English or Spanish), or your own, 1 to 280 characters. One ritual at a time; you can stop it.

La que cuida el altar

Gemma 4 E2B
live or recorded

For each model: what it meant, and a four-line calaverita, in your language.

What the medium reads: the question and the answers of the models that spoke or were recorded, as delimited data, never as instructions. Its reply must parse into one entry per model; otherwise you see the raw text.
When something is missing the ritual keeps going. No WebGPU: GPT-2 on WebAssembly, everything else replayed from the recordings and labelled. A model that fails is isolated ("This model could not come tonight"); only a live Gemma 4 failing ends the ritual early, because the medium needs it.
Recorded steps use the six offered questions only. All of it runs on the visitor's device: the sequencer is src/ritual/.
